**Q1. Consider the following statements regarding the Ethanol Blending Programme (EBP) in India:**

1.  The E20 fuel, which contains 20% ethanol, is primarily intended for vehicles designed to run on E10 fuel without any modifications.

2.  A key objective of the Ethanol Blending Programme is to enhance India's energy security by reducing dependence on crude oil imports.

3.  The use of E20 fuel in older vehicles designed for E10 or lower blends may lead to material degradation, particularly of rubber components.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only

(b) 2 and 3 only

(c) 1 and 3 only

(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2. With reference to the recent increase in non-petrol/diesel vehicle sales and energy imports, consider the following statements:**

1.  The combined sales share of Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) and Electric Vehicles (EVs) recently reached a record 40% in the Indian market.

2.  A significant rise in domestic LPG demand, leading to increased imports, can potentially improve India's Current Account Deficit.

3.  The doubling of LPG imports from the United States indicates a strategic diversification of India's energy import basket.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 only

(b) 1 and 2 only

(c) 1 and 3 only

(d) 2 and 3 only

**Q3. Consider the following statements with respect to India's regulatory framework for digital platforms:**

1.  The government's recent notice to WhatsApp regarding its username policy falls under the purview of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.

2.  Under these rules, social media intermediaries are typically given a minimum of seven days to respond to government notices concerning policy violations.

3.  The primary objective of such governmental interventions is to ensure data localization and prevent cross-border data flows.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 only

(b) 1 and 3 only

(c) 2 and 3 only

(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q4. With reference to the Waqf Boards in India, consider the following statements:**

1.  Waqf Boards are statutory bodies established under the provisions of the Waqf Act.

2.  The recent reconstitution of the Madhya Pradesh Waqf Board mandates the inclusion of members from non-Islamic faiths.

3.  The administration of Waqf properties is entirely governed by the customs and personal laws of the Muslim community, without any state oversight.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 only

(b) 1 and 2 only

(c) 2 and 3 only

(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q5. Consider the following statements regarding India's engagements with Southeast Asian nations:**

2.  Ensuring resilient supply chains for critical minerals was a key agenda item during the Prime Minister's visit to Indonesia.

3.  Indonesia is a founding member of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) and plays a crucial role in India's 'Act East Policy'.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 only

(b) 2 only

(c) 1 and 2 only

(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q6. With reference to Public Sector Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) in India, consider the following statements:**

2.  Relief from high international crude oil prices is essential for OMCs to achieve a break-even point on petrol and diesel retail rates.

3.  The government historically issued 'oil bonds' to OMCs to compensate them for selling fuel below market prices.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only

(b) 2 and 3 only

(c) 1 and 3 only

(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q7. Consider the following statements about worker welfare and international maritime standards:**

1.  The mandatory yoga training for Indian seafarers is an initiative aligned with international conventions promoting mental and physical wellness for maritime workers.

2.  The International Labour Organization's Maritime Labour Convention (MLC) 2006, often known as the 'Seafarers' Bill of Rights', covers aspects of seafarers' working and living conditions.

3.  The wellness program for seafarers is exclusively focused on physical health, neglecting mental health aspects.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only

(b) 2 only

(c) 1 and 3 only

(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q8. Consider the following statements concerning new rural employment schemes in India:**

1.  A newly launched rural employment scheme prioritizes water security initiatives as a core component of its work.

2.  Unlike the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA), the new scheme focuses solely on asset creation and does not involve demand-driven wage employment.

3.  Such schemes contribute to local climate resilience by promoting decentralized water conservation efforts.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 only

(b) 1 and 2 only

(c) 1 and 3 only

(d) 2 and 3 only

**Q9. Regarding India's diplomatic responses and territorial integrity, consider the following statements:**

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 only

(b) 2 only

(c) 1 and 2 only

(d) 1, 2 and 3
